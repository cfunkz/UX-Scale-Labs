When to use it

When you care about the whole experience, not only usability, and want to benchmark against other products.

How it is scored

  1. Each answer is transformed to −3 (most negative) … +3 (most positive). Half the pairs start with the positive term and are reversed.
  2. Scale scores are item means. Values between −0.8 and +0.8 are read as neutral (UEQ handbook).
  3. Benchmark categories (Excellent, Good, Above average, Below average, Bad) use the general UEQ benchmark published in the handbook.
  4. The handbook advises reporting the six scales separately rather than a single overall number.

Subscales

  • Attractiveness · items 1, 12, 14, 16, 24, 25
  • Perspicuity · items 2, 4, 13, 21
  • Efficiency · items 9, 20, 22, 23
  • Dependability · items 8, 11, 17, 19
  • Stimulation · items 5, 6, 7, 18
  • Novelty · items 3, 10, 15, 26

Reverse-scored items

3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 12, 17, 18, 19, 21, 23, 24, 25. Participants are not told which items are reversed.

Frequently asked questions about the UEQ

How do I calculate the UEQ score?

  1. Each answer is transformed to −3 (most negative) … +3 (most positive). Half the pairs start with the positive term and are reversed.
  2. Scale scores are item means. Values between −0.8 and +0.8 are read as neutral (UEQ handbook).
  3. Benchmark categories (Excellent, Good, Above average, Below average, Bad) use the general UEQ benchmark published in the handbook.
  4. The handbook advises reporting the six scales separately rather than a single overall number.

This calculator applies these steps automatically as soon as every item is answered.

What is a good UEQ score?

Each scale runs from −3 to +3. Values above +0.8 are positive and below −0.8 negative. Against the UEQ benchmark, a scale counts as excellent above roughly 1.6 to 2.0, depending on the scale.

How long does the UEQ take?

The UEQ has 26 items and takes about 4 minutes. Typical use: When you care about the whole experience, not only usability, and want to benchmark against other products.

Can I calculate UEQ scores for many participants at once?

Yes. Collect the answers in Excel, Google Forms, Qualtrics or any other tool with one row per participant and columns Q1 to Q26, then upload the table as CSV or JSON on the "Score a dataset" tab. If participants filled in the UEQ here and sent you their downloads, drop all the PDF, CSV, JSON or ZIP files in at once instead. You get a score for every person, plus the mean, standard deviation and 95% confidence interval.

What is similar to the UEQ?

Similar questionnaires include User Experience Questionnaire – Short (UEQ-S), System Usability Scale (SUS), Visual Aesthetics of Websites Inventory – Short (VisAWI-S).
