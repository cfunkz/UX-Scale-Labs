Frequently asked questions about the SEQ

How do I calculate the SEQ score?

  1. The score is the response itself (1–7).
  2. Across hundreds of tasks the average SEQ is roughly 5.5 (Sauro & Lewis, 2016), which is used as the reference point.

This calculator applies these steps automatically as soon as every item is answered.

What is a good SEQ score?

Across hundreds of tasks the average SEQ is about 5.5. Tasks that score below roughly 4.5 are usually worth investigating.

How long does the SEQ take?

The SEQ has 1 item and takes about 12 seconds. Typical use: Immediately after every task in a usability test, so you can compare task difficulty.

Can I calculate SEQ scores for many participants at once?

Yes. Collect the answers in Excel, Google Forms, Qualtrics or any other tool with one row per participant and columns Q1 to Q1, then upload the table as CSV or JSON on the "Score a dataset" tab. If participants filled in the SEQ here and sent you their downloads, drop all the PDF, CSV, JSON or ZIP files in at once instead. You get a score for every person, plus the mean, standard deviation and 95% confidence interval.

What is similar to the SEQ?

Similar questionnaires include After-Scenario Questionnaire (ASQ), NASA Task Load Index (Raw TLX), System Usability Scale (SUS).
