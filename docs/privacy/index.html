Privacy policy

I don’t collect any personal data. Your questionnaire answers, scores and uploaded files stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere. The only third party that sets cookies here is Google, through Google AdSense and Google Analytics, as explained below.

Your answers and files

All scoring happens on your own device. Nothing you type in, and no file you upload (a dataset or a batch of participants’ result files), is sent to a server or stored anywhere. Close the tab and it’s gone. Exports (PDF, CSV, JSON) are created in your browser and saved straight to your device.

The site remembers one thing in your browser’s local storage: whether you picked the light or dark theme. It also keeps a copy of its own pages, styles, scripts and fonts in your browser’s cache so the next visit loads instantly and works offline. That cache holds only the site’s files, never your answers, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, which may be based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how they perform. This site has no access to those cookies, and the ad code can’t see your answers.

If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you’ll be asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time. Anyone can turn off personalised ads in Google Ad Settings. Google explains how it uses this data in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

Google Analytics counts page visits and three actions: finishing a questionnaire, scoring a dataset and exporting results. Each action only records the questionnaire’s name and the file type, never answers or scores. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or any content blocker.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may briefly log your IP address like any web server. Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Questions
