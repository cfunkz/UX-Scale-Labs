Stats tools for UX research

The statistics that come up in almost every usability study or thesis: how many participants to recruit, whether design B really beat design A, and how to report completion rates, task times and reliability properly.

Each tool follows the methods in Sauro and Lewis’s Quantifying the User Experience and gives you a sentence you can paste into a report.
