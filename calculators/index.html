Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a usability score?

Pick a standardised questionnaire such as the System Usability Scale, collect the answers, and apply its scoring rules: for SUS that means adjusting each item, adding them up and multiplying by 2.5 to get a score from 0 to 100. Each calculator here does this automatically and compares the result with published benchmarks.

How do I calculate NPS, CSAT or CES?

NPS is the percentage of promoters (9–10) minus the percentage of detractors (0–6). CSAT is the percentage of 4 and 5 ratings on a five-point scale. CES 2.0 is the mean of a seven-point agreement rating, often reported with the share of 5–7 answers. The NPS, CSAT and CES calculators work these out for one response or a whole dataset.

Can I calculate survey results for many respondents at once?

Yes. Every questionnaire calculator has a Score a dataset tab that takes a CSV or JSON file with one row per person, and Combine results merges the individual result files participants download. Both give each person’s score, the mean, a 95% confidence interval and reliability.

How many participants do I need?

It depends on whether you want to find problems, estimate a score or compare designs. Five users find most common problems, around 50 give a SUS score within ±5 points, and comparisons usually need more. The sample size calculator works it out for your own numbers.

Is my data saved or uploaded?

No. Every calculator runs in your browser, so answers and files are never sent to a server. Close the tab and they are gone.
